What this will become

A permanent, freely accessible archive of Bob Randolph's body of work: every poem he wrote, given a page of its own, in his own words and in his own hand where a page survives to show it. The plan, as it stands:

Text

Every poem transcribed to HTML for searching and reading, paired with a toggle to view the original scanned page — so nothing is silently "corrected" out of Bob's own spelling, line breaks, or layout. Organized chapbook by chapbook, the way Bob himself published them, with the full 1,775-poem master index underneath as the complete catalogue.

Audio & Video first clip live

Extemporaneous B-roll of Bob doing live readings — the archive's centerpiece, being digitized off the original tapes clip by clip. The first clip is up on the Taped Live Readings page. His documentary photography is a second body of work in its own right, not just illustration for the poems — see Photography.

Print planned

A print-on-demand edition of Bob's seminal work, The Picture of Dorian America, edited by Mark Wotton, with its own ISBN, so the collection enters library and bookstore catalogs the way a traditionally published book would.

Translation planned

Every poem page is already built to hold more than one language side by side — the language switcher on each poem page is live now, waiting for translated text. English is the only language populated so far.
