A Working Archive — Prototype

This is a working prototype — built from the material catalogued so far. Poems here are rendered from Bob's own pages.

This will become

A permanent, freely accessible archive of Bob Randolph's body of work: every poem he wrote, given a page of its own, in his own words and in his own hand where a page survives to show it. The plan, as it stands:

Text

Every poem transcribed to HTML for searching and reading, paired with a toggle to view the original scanned page — so nothing is silently “corrected” out of Bob's own spelling, line breaks, or layout. Organized chapbook by chapbook, the way Bob himself published them, with the full 1,775-poem master index underneath as the complete catalogue.

Audio & Video

Extemporaneous source video of Bob doing live readings — digitized off the original tapes clip by clip, linked at the Taped Live Readings page. Select documentary photos drawn from nearly 3,000 pictures Bob took in his travels around the world — a second body of work in its own right, not just illustration for the poems — see Photography.

Print planned

A print-on-demand edition of Bob's seminal work, The Picture of Dorian America, edited by Mark Wotton, with its own ISBN, so the collection enters library and bookstore catalogs the way a traditionally published book would.

Translation planned

Every poem page is already built to hold more than one language side by side — the language switcher on each poem page is live, waiting for translated text. English is the only language populated so far.

Start here

Yokohama Rickshaw Boy — the first chapbook added with live poem pages. Or browse the full 1,775-poem master index, Bob's own numbered and coded catalogue of everything he wrote.
