Mary Thompson

Christmas Day, 1932 - Grandma's big dinner in the old house on LeConte Street in Hollywood. You were 70ish and a nurse who knew how to put a hot bottle on the boil on my 12-year-old neck, to draw it all out - You've forgotten, yet it stood out for me, an event of caring now floating in my life., secure in eternal time, so everything - at least from Christmas 1932 is alive and safe in my universal memory. Where are you, Mary Thompson - now in time and space? Are you hearing me? I would like to kiss your cheek... to let you know I still remember....
