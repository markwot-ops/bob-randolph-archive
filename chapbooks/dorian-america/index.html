The Picture of Dorian America

Poems by Bob Randolph · selected and edited by Mark Wotton.

The edited hardcover selection Mark drew from Bob's work and prepared for print under Bob's own imprint, Randolph House Press — never actually released. This is the finished book itself: cover, dedication, Tom Sanders' introduction, Bob's preface, the “letter to my parents,” the family photographs, all 72 poems in Bob's own layout, and the closing photograph — page for page, as he made it.
