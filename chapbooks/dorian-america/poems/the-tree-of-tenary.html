The Tree of Tenary

At one place in the vast Sahara is one tree - at Tenary, where ages past the earth's crust sprang one tiny leak and let water on this tiny place. And then one thousand years - one bird dropped one seed beside this pool within its meager mud, to germinate and grow to leaves and roots - to be one day a fragile tree in all this space, to be at least one guide for camel caravans across this sandy sea. We salute you, tree of Tenary! Tell us once again just how it went, just how you did all this... Then tell us where we go from here...
