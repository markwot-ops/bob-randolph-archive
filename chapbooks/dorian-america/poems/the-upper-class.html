The upper class

The beautiful older woman from Venezuela, her expressive, open face, and her daughter who looks so much like her - and her son-in-law, who smiles at his wife a couple of times a day but who is basically a very handsome Latin American nincompoop with a stiff graceful slow looking straight ahead kind of military walk and his arms hanging limp and straight down, who looks like he's too distinguished to shine his shoes but doesn't object to feeling a little love for his wife or his mother-in-law when they shine them in good spirits so he'll look nice and the amazing thing is they are loving and open in such a way that they don't even notice he's basically a 12 year old nincompoop who now is 35 living in the Venezuelan nincompoop upper class.
