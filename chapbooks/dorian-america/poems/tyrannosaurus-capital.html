Tyrannosaurus capital

All the cells in a dinosaur's body are innocent. They don't know what the whole body is doing. Even brain cells don't realize things - individually. No single brain cell ever had its own thought all by itself, a thought not shared with other cells. It's toughest of all in the case of Tyrannesaurus Rex. His and her name means the king or queen of tyranny. Gigantic teeth to rip hearts and stomachs and eyeballs out of more peaceful reptiles back in the Mezozoic, in their own hundred million years before little egg-eating mammals the size of a dog or a pig came along or some super-meteorite that collided with earth and threw up dust that gave them the first nuclear winter at 40 below or both happened - maybe. Remember pictures you've seen of Rex, teeth a foot long made of dentyn and enamel and whatever else teeth are made of - all of individual cells that can't see or understand or hear, maybe they can feel - like a toothache. Some tooth cells are on the cutting edge - like stock brokers in Tyrannesaurus Capital. Making deals, setting up buyouts, first of Ma and Pa's Waffle House on Spring Street, because the land was valuable and the old dump would be torn down - gobbled up. Then buyouts of capital companies, lightbulb companies, tractor companies, wheat marketing companies, then capital countries, like England, Brazil, Russia, Yugoslavia and Japan. Buy them ! Now, before somebody else does ! And not one cell knew the consequences, a cell in a Rex tooth holds firm to do its job - to help the whole system devour an agricultural country, a whole vegetarian brontesaurus. Every cell wants to survive and get a paycheck so it can cell-divide and keep in shape. Cells want to survive - like us. We're cells. Our white blood cells have to give their lives, like soldiers to save the whole body, and they're as ignorant of the politics and economics of the body as soldiers are ignorant of the body politic. But you see, a whole flesh-eating capital heavy country can do incredible damage to others, and to cells in its own body, but it's like tides or glaciers, you can hardly see what's happening. You as a cell can't tell, you're just trying to survive. But your whole dinosasur -- your whole country may be a tyrant, a gangster, a bloody butcher, a cannibal, an executioner in the world of other huge beings, of other huge systems. But you're just a cell, in muscle or brain or tooth tissue and you just want to survive. You have no global view of the behaviour of the dinosaur system of which you are a part with the blood and gore of others dripping down the teeth down the very tooth where you are - where you are a stock broker, a lawyer, a soldier or a certified public accountant on the cutting edge. You are a cell in, a citizen of Tyrannesaurus Capital, perhaps the last giant system, the last dinosaur before extinction.
