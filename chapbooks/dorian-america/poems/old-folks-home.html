Old folks' home

One in a remedial chair faces the door, her face round jowels squaring the face, staring blankly; light blue blouse, necklace - no one to talk to her daughter, Connie, left a half hour ago, leaving her to think back when she and Will married then Connie was born and the church the choir, the walks in the woods, the family gatherings like yesterday. No one to talk to. Everyone is gone. Lillian pours over her bible at her desk. Always there, in profile. Her back shaped like a letter C. White hair stacked up. All day, Lillian and her bible. She knows she's old but the other night she dreamed she saw herself nude on a little beach by the lake with her first boy friend, Edgar, also nude. She woke up that night in disbelief, bewildered to be in an old people's home. She was dazed til dawn. Margaret walks up and down the halls, sometimes with her walker. Her dress hikes up in back, her beads clatter against her walker in a certain rhythm, making her think of the high school band she once was in. She laughs to herself, remembering funny stuff on TV. She had very nice breasts, once. Now? She can't do much with them. Besides, the men there are all so old and just sit in the hall leaning against the wall watching TV by the nurses' station, til they fall asleep. She'd like to write to her children, especially the oldest. What was his name? Teddy? Yes. He looks just like his dad. But Phil's been gone so long. It's hard to believe. Old Billy is 83. He lives peacefully in a lovely cloud. He never could think of death. He's in room 277, all to himself. Eleanor still wishes she could go to college. She's in 239A, a shared room. A sensitive spirit, able to feel angry at attendants when they are harsh at times.. She is my first wife and she is still bright and thoughtful and lovely and I am proud of her. Ben, everybody calls him Benny, wants to talk politics all the time. He likes the old ladies and still gets an erection when he has to go to the toilet in the mornings. He's been trying to get the world to be a better place for 60 years and he's getting disgusted, he likes Millie and Sheryl best, sort of, but he can't figure out what to do about it. And then his kids never come by, he might as well let the ladies think he's a bachelor or a widower or something but they just smile at him and go back to sleep in front of TV or have to go to the bathroom. So he switches to politics to work off steam. Then Margaret Golby, not the other one, and Arthur. Everyone smiles at their little affair. Once in a while they've sneaked a little kiss, looking sideways and they put their hands on each other's shoulder blades and everyone clicks their tongue and says 'oh my', at these goings on but it livens up the day and most of the women aren't very jealous. Billy the only other man on the wing is still in his lovely pink world and doesn't notice any of this. Of special interest is the sneaky little sight of them patting each other on the behind once in a while, mostly him patting her on the behind. Then the best of all was when Amy saw Arthur coming out of Margaret's room at 4 AM one morning, which became a very important occasion for everyone that could, to say "Oh, my goodness", and several of the women had dreams the next week or two that were sort of - well sort of erotic. But you see - if you just visited here once or twice you would think you were seeing old people, older men and women who were in decay, their lives nearly at an end, little to live for, their best years their vibrant years their sexual years far far behind. unless somehow you were able to plumb to your own lower depths and see the wondrous quality of deeper older life, and you would hear the inner youthfulness that lasts, almost to the very end.
