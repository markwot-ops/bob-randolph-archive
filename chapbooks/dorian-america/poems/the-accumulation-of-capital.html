The accumulation of capital

Capitalism is built on the most rapid possible accumulation of capital. Human well-being is based on the most patient delicate tender steady imaginative accumulation of love. Capital is the machinery, the factory buildings, the security guards, the steel and iron and marble and concrete, the jagged edges, the metal powder in the air, the rust in the gutters, the two inch wide supplies, the 4000 pound supplies, the 4 million screws, the plastic parts that won't rot but will crack, the foam rubber parts that won't crack but will rot, all this to take precedence over human laughter, body fluids and church choirs, precedence over backpacking on wild trails overlooking oceans, with soaring birds overhead, with small mammals surviving in the tall grass and watching us precedence over backpacking on wild trails carefully. But do not forget, whatever you do, if you want to be able to make your mortgage payments and buy liquor for your office parties, remember that power and employment are based on the most rapid possible accumulation of capital -- of the spread of concrete, plastic and laser beams, and have nothing to do with soft skin, eyelashes and tender lips.
