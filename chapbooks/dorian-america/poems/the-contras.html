The contras

The contras are poverty-stricken peasants who never learned how to read and needed a job and Gilberto told you about money and food and clothes and good shoes for once, and guns but they taught you how to use them and some priests who spoke native Spanish gave you guidance and took your confessions and told you the bible says how evil the government and the communist farms are that took land from the good people who took good wild care of the land by owning it for generations in the name of the Lord Jesus and Mary the mother of God, and at first you didn't feel good shooting other peasants with other soldiers dressed like you, but you learned you had to to save the country from the communist devils who were crucifying the Lord every day and you knew that was true because sometimes when you were resting under a tree your rosary beads spoke to you and told you to keep the faith
