Minus One

When the lightning flashes with the earth, the tale we're told is that the sky is minus one. When my heart can pound and skip a beat, the tale I'm told is that my life is minus one. When I go to sleep and only dream of you, my spirit knows my night is minus one. The sound of minus one the feel of minus one the pulse of minus one the time of minus one Minus one Minus one To find the balance of my life is minus one Oh, tell my heart to hold me tight to light my pain of minus one. When laughter in the night can fill the vessels of our lives can take us deeply in its arms and let us see we're minus one. Across the mountains near the dawn A coyote's cry now seeking for his mate conveys the sound of minus one A yellow flower opening wide beneath the morning sun is reaching out for pollinating life to fill and still its minus one. The nursing baby the puppy or the cub, that cry for milk as best they can instinctively know the time has come to fill its minus one. My heart can break for minus one. The great elm spreading tall each yellow leaf will fall in its own turn, telling others thay are minus one. the best of minus one the pulse of minus one the life of minus one the death of minus one tha last love of minus one the first love of minus one so few condors left to soar on 8 foot wings, their nearing end caused by man's embrace of earth and air, this whole specie now nearly minus one. ancient minus one newborn minus one cloud shapes minus one minus one has been the key to understand for each end each death each season come and gone all change all life all time all are the sum of minus one yet for each lack each loss of minus one there are newer ones in their turn and time, that for now and eternally are a gain of one, the triumphant birth of one, the fertile time of one, let each time of minus one be rhythm's turn to keep it whole delicately balanced delicately poised at one and one and minus one, the womb is filled and when the babe is born, the mother finds herself feeling the loss of minus one
