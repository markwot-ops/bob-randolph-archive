A 2nd class Mexican bus

2 crates of broccoli across the aisle 3 chickens hanging upside down from a broomstick in the back soft faces, peaceful beautiful faces young men in white shirts not tucked in and the top buttons open straw peasant hats a voice and a guitar sing in the back 3 guys and 2 saguaros at a desert stop one has a guitar, one still peeing beside a saguaro, first water it's had in weeks on the bus everyone waits, talking and laughing everyone, including the bus driver patiently wait for the guy to finish they all know the feeling - a full bladder on a bumpy bus Next time you're in Mexico be sure to ride a 2nd class Mexican bus -- to somewhere . . . . .
