A hollowness in his profile

Good looking young man who looks older and worn, a discouraged slope to his shoulders, a hollowness in his profile. Short beard, looking unwashed. His eyes cast about - which way to go? He eventually crosses the street. There is no destination, no goal, because this morning he did not come from anywhere, his roots have shriveled and let him drift on the wind into the world.
