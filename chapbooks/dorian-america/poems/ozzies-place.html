Ozzie's Place

Not too many investors have tuna salad sandwiches prepared by hand with a teaspoon, stirred on a narrow counter's edge... There's no "futures" in it. How do you capitalize a grilled cheese and a milkshake made the old-fashioned way? by hand... by a little man who laughs with the customers like our grandparents did decades ago? There's nowhere near enough profit in this operation, and too many jokes and too much laughter at lunchtime, and it's not very dignified... Obsolete home-made salads made by an obsolete little man at an obsolete soda fountain in an obsolete pharmacy in obsolete Elmwood, with an obsolete shoe repair shop and an obsolete appliance repair shop down the street; where you can really get your toasters and flatirons and electric fans fixed by obsolete guys who look for obsolete parts - instead of telling you to trash the old thing and get the new one with 24 buttons and computer chips to do the job... But back to Ozzie's place - Somebody ought to modernize things so 20 hamburgers can be fixed at once on an IBM computer oven with old women in tacky uniforms at minimum wage, serving lines of folks with Have a Good Day, and nobody laughing and nobody knowing anybody, but maybe it will be so profitable that Ozzie's place could be franchised and go nationwide. Then it will be worth buying by an insurance company or an investment group, and maybe even be exported to China and Australia and Las Vegas and Milpitas and serve millions and make millions, and finally be big time for Ozzie's place - instead of just people meeting at Russell and College because they know each other and want to have a deeply rich hour together in the middle of the day - So...., Ozzie's like me, with his little containers of sandwich spreads and his spoons and his apron and his slightly bent-over figure making things; lovingly... like he was massaging them to bring them alive...
