Women in prison, it serves them right...

Children don't behave worth a damn, so it serves them right to have their mothers in prison. It serves the mothers right, they stole baby food from a 7-11 store. It serves them right, they never bothered to get an education and get some skills, especially when they lost their jobs when their corporation employer they washed windows and vacuumed carpets for before they closed down and moved to Mexico. Then when her boy friend got drunk and got arrested and she couldn't pay the rent or work with two kids and she ended up on the streets doped with some crack a "friend" gave her before he screwed her. So it's not the system's fault. It never heard of her. And the corporation? - she was unknown, she was just a window washer. They never heard of her either - not really. So she was bad news, a real loser and she hardly spoke English. Lock her up so she'll get some food and shelter and can't get drugs because she was so depressed - here in the home of the brave and the land of the free - and she'll be able to work in the prison making plastic cups or papers clips at 40 cents an hour - that's better than nothing. And there's medical care, over at Chowchilla and it's only 36 miles and she can make an appointment when she can afford a phone call and can just go right on over when she can get a seat on the bus - so what the hell, she's way better off in prison - right? Menstruation in prison? They know about it. They'll send a man guard around with 10 or 20 tampons, who probably won't make a snide remark. But just think, when you have no control over the processes of your life, suppose your period is a huge one or you have a yeast infection, or diarrhea or bleeding gums, or a kidney stone, you might as well be a carton of old books, or an old jacket with holes in the elbows, that's about as important as you are, when you're in prison. How do you use a vibrator in your sexually-deprived life? How do you read up on child care? How do you get postcards to send to your children you haven't seen since a year ago April? How do you do anything that is beautiful, that is human? that will help you function and make a living when you're back outside? Well, at least you can work for corporations now while you're in prison. You can assemble electronic parts, raise pigs, make license plates, do telemarketing, stuff mattresses, slaughter ostriches for their feathers, be reservation clerks for TWA Airlines so they can transfer clerks to flight attendant jobs to break a flight attendant strike. And your wages? Minimum or less. $4.25 or less, but after deductions they take home about 95 cents. At least it's more than in Latin America or eastern Europe or Asia, those great continental global prisons. Then some prisons charge rent from prisoners making money on the outside. Can you believe it?
