"Troops pass atomic test"

(Title from a newspaper clipping — S.F. News, Monday, June 2, 1952: "Troops pass atomic test, / Can attack safely after A-Bomb blast.") Yes, they passed the test, they could attack into the high radiation zone without effect. The effects wouldn't appear until 20 years had passed and hair had fallen out of half their heads and one third had liver cancer, 40% had lymphoma, a fourth had prostate, rectal, foreskin or brain cancer. They would be okay. Only 74% would die before they were 60. Not bad considering the stakes the word is always attack. There's always somebody with a big stick, a big gun, a big dick, and they want everything possible The word is "attack." The troops passed the test, that day at least. It was on a Sunday, the day of our Lord, the day of the nuclear Lord, June 1, 1952. Not a single soldier had a single problem during June. Only a few problems showed up in July, just minor skin lesions, or dizziness or vomiting, aspirin could fix easy. Troops pass atomic test. They can attack. No problem. World War III will be over by the time we're covered with skin lesions and they'll be all healed before World War IV, and by then it'll all be over because we'll own everything and pay wages to Asia and Africa and Latin America and we'll have all the surplus and we'll always be able to attack with nuclear cannisters and cash registers and stock certificates cancer and the troops pass atomic test attack attack skin cancer breast cancer attack attack bone cancer, the troops pass atomic test with no visible damage so every day of every week because now we know the troops will be American and they'll be able to pass the atomic test pass the atomic test. So . . . , you pacifists, we're running this show, and we know what we're doin'. You'll see the troops are passed they're all passed they've all passed and we still attack right up to the end - "The troops pass atomic test."
