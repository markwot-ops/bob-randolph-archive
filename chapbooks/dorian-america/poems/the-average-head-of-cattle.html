The average head of cattle

The average head of cattle pretty much eats all the time. It eats grass on Tuesdays and Thursdays. I call it "it" because if it was once male it has had its balls lopped off and if it was female it has been defiled in some similar way. Next Wednesday at 3:15 pm it will be eating grass. At a quarter to six and also at a quarter to eleven it will be eating grass. All January it will be standing around - mostly, during the freezing nights wondering ever so slightly why it is so cold. Next March it will be eating grass on Mondays and Fridays, and also the rest of the week. During the first 30 days of September I suppose it will be eating grass. Then one day it will go over somewhere on a truck to a big building where it will have its brains smashed in - just like that. Wouldn't you imagine that by this time at least one cattle would look around a little thoughtfully at its fellows before it is too late, and say "Hey, does anybody here have any idea what this is all about?"
