These cities of the damned

They died in their decade in their century seeking fresh water, loving arms, holding children and other lovers through the night, rest and peace and honor for everyone in every place every boy and girl every gorgeous woman every grizzled elder, that they all might feel the warmth of love the comfort of shelter the excitement of passion of mating in the midst of pillows on great beds, of laughter on merry-go-rounds in every neighborhood. Of picnics by white crystal streams, symphonies, 2-act plays, 3-act plays, music, poetry at dawn and at midnight, children's choruses, grandmothers with healthy skin and friendly smiles, all this beauty god-like from the goddess, like fragments and portions of life on every beach, on every flower with every butterfly on every ornamented slope. In every language, every skin color, wrinkled brow, every child with permanent teeth. Yet in spite of all this, the 500,000 in 1965 floating dead down rivers in Indonesia, on portions of every continent, every island, the headless bodies of young boys, blood-stained thighs of dead young girls, skeletal remains of starved brown-skin babies all dead behind screams and moans -- caused by the troops, the tanks the bombardments, poison gases, the torpedoes, rockets, depth bombs, the shards of hell spraying in every innocent village where copper or iron ore are underground nearby. With machine guns mounted on Jeep Cherokees and grimy torture chambers behind stucco walls near the ruins of ancient Aztex, Maya or Inca civilizations. Then to kill in 20 minutes with poisons both orange and green, to get the peasants hauled away by earth-moving monsters that can push over giant trees 2000 years old when necessary; or scrape peasants and villages to the side. So for the present the deadly power wins, with all the murdered ones disposed of, those who dreamed and played guitars and flutes for children, and combed the hair of lovely elders in rest homes. These most lovable of ourselves, that had to be eliminated for the sake of profit and enterprise - erased, murdered gotten out the way, out of the way of profit-making machinery that tears up, grinds out and pummels paper dollars into savings accounts. So all the removed, the dispatched, the cancelled, the erased, the dead, their echoes of laughter, music and gorgeous dancing now haunt the paths and alleys of these cities, these heroic cities of the murdered, these cities of the damned.
