Old Macedonian Woman

Macedonia's memories holding sway Over years of rhythm's joys denied. The graceful beat makes new the gaze from wrinkled eyes, The toothless smiles, with faint remembered kisses on rocky meadows, Savored long ago, though custom said A woman's passion must in silence be. All rediscovered in the dancing figures serpentining by, So close to feel the heat of motion, out of time and yet in time. Her ancestral peasant bed, receiving , giving without knowing, And then to dance with babe in arms, to give her milk to any child. To learn thereby her power over life, To make a gift of joyful light Shed from her soul on those nearby, With all the knowing echoes found in twilight's time. Her stunted dreams have called us home at last, To tell us that our age is but our youth in fuller flight, An upward, soaring cadence - without end.
