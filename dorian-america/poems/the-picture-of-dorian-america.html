The Picture of Dorian America

It was a gorgeous portrait of the young republic, as described by the new leaders in the late 1700s.. It had a wide smile, flashing teeth, bright steely blue eyes. The posture was erect, head held high. It was dressed in a proud light blue uniform coat, its long rifle beside it. It was a picture of Dorian America, exhibited by one of the new powerful land owners, an early member of the burgeoning class of money, ownership and power, in this fledgling continent. Years passed, the seasons turned, rivers flooded and horse-drawn carts crossed small bridges in the hills, with oil lamps shining into the night through rough-hewn windows in the villages. . The Indians, the aborigines of the new continent were a shadowy people, lurking in the night, watching the new world being formed. Wagon trains each spring travelled west, wresting more land; forests and plains from Indian tribes. Each year's Thanksgiving was the occasion to visit this Dorian portrait, to enable all to keep the beauty of the country's birth in view, to renew their revolutionary spirit and to look forward into the rising future. Only great leaders of the growing commerce were included in the viewing of the forebear, Dorian. Am I imagining it, but is the mouth beginning to curl into a twisted corner on the left side? I will watch for this, remember, to see if it is true, a twist, so early in the history of the country? I notice others looking startled as they stare into that face, of Dorian, our forefather. So weeks passed and became months and merchants talked with each other amiably in front of their stores and the railroad was pushed through the poorer neighborhood by the creek, and runaway slaves were slipping through town at night heading north and once in a while one was lynched but not very often and the weekly town newspaper kept quiet about it. In the hills of Kentucky and West Virginia coal mines were sunk but casualties were high and they found out about carbon monoxide and how canaries helped but on August 23rd, a hot day there was a mine explosion and 86 workers were buried and it left the families destitute because even when the miners were working their families were in poverty, and the government didn't help because it was a free country, and many could not afford school clothes or shoes for their children and they had to have the kids work in the fields to help pay the bills. Through it all politicians and preachers praised the Lord and the wonderful new free country and they decided on the 4th of July and Washington's Birthday and Labor Day in addition to Thanksgiving. When the 4th of July arrived, they gathered at the room where the picture was. As they walked in, in the dim light - the face, the face . . . gleamed with light brown light but the teeth they were longer, protruding more, with wrinkles around the eyes, the hair and eyebrows needed trimming. The whole group seemed stunned and stood in silence. How could their America look like this? What pact with Satan had someone made? The Baptist preacher and the mayor looked at each other. Neither spoke. Then the mayor turned away and outside he took a deep breath and reminded the others about the parade that afternoon - and be in front of their stores to greet people. It was an important day, to celebrate their growth and their promising future. Nothing was said that day about the Picture of Dorian America. And so, great events came and went, the Civil War, when the theft of Africans from their continent to be free labor for this new paradise democracy, the United States of America, and slavery was abolished, but negroes, African blacks were still being assaulted in every way, cheated, not able to be citizens, to vote, to live in decent housing, but this was never very important in this terrific new country because it was almost never talked about anywhere I t was a good place, towns often said "some of their best friends were niggers". And lynchings, if a young black happened to glance at a white woman, and this was noticed, he might be hanged for rape. Then depressions came, financial panics, but the country always recovered - eventually. Now there were 4 visits to the Dorian America picture, but every time it was different, more disturbing, more deformed, one time the hair was a gray green, the clothing was torn, a front tooth was chipped, it looked like blood on the shirt, the eyes did not shine and were cloudy. Few important people in the town went any more to see the Picture of Dorian America , because it was haunting. They were frightened by the changes they saw - it was black magic. Wars came and went, and veterans were an important part of the population, especially in the 4th of July, and Armistice Day parades celebrating the end of World War I. Then the depression of the 30s, the abject despair after the euphoria of the 20s, families going west in ramshackle old cars, with mattresses and suitcases strapped on the side. Then Roosevelt and his work programs, farm help programs, but these faded after a time, then everything was rescued by World War II. In this war the countries of Europe tore each other to shreds, with the United States relatively untouched, and so could dictate much, but always disguised as to the real intent The Marshall Plan, yes, seemed so magnanimous, but we were not told the ruined countries had to spend the loans with American corporations. So - so - then, the Korean War, after both North and South had crossed the 38th parallel dozens of times, the North was finally lured into invading south and after several years the war approached the Chinese border, where there had been a communist revolution in 1949, so maybe there was a connection. Who knows, because soon it was made clear that East was East and West was West and never the twain, even through the United Nations, crippled by its veto for the powerful ones, could meet. So let's get on with war production because everyone knew we would have to fight our "ally", the Soviet Union, that had borne the brunt of loss, death and damage on our side in World War II, for example, 20 million dead at the hands of the Germans, but that didn't make any difference, we had to protect the world from insidious forces that would change the market economy that we knew worked so well everywhere under our leadership. But then the Russians invaded Czechosovakia, and all we have done since World War II, was invade the Dominican Republic, Grenada, Cuba, Lebanon, to bomb Iraq, get troops in Iran, invade Viet Nam, bomb Cambodia and Laos, overthrow Nicaragua, El Salvador, and Guatemala, kill the presidents of the Congo and Chile, break up Yugoslavia, seemingly doing fine, then get troops in Bosnia, Serbia and Croatia, Montenegro and Herzgovina and, and finally bomb Serbia into the 4th century B.C. Then with sanctions and controlled air space, completely dominate their death squad governments, who in Venezuela and Colombia under U.S. control began the shipments of massive amounts of drugs into American cities to keep American youth from becoming revolutionaries, bomb Panama, and most of all, drive the Soviet Union into bankruptcy trying to keep up with our bombs, our bombers, and nuclear rockets, all aimed at them just in case, of course, always just in case. Our 50,000 nuclear bombs, our 37 submarines each with over 400 nuclear warheads on board, our aircraft carriers, with bombs and planes and torpedoes on all 7 oceans, all weapons of peace, the whole world an armed camp, armed with American weapons, aimed now at everybody. So, finally, after a few years a party of American leaders, with pride wanted to take a look at the Picture of Dorian America. After years of warfare, on each continent, they remembered the Picture. They had not seen it for years. They turned back at the door, in the dim light, for what they saw, oh, I can hardly tell you, what they saw was Dorian America sitting in his place, a submachine gun across his lap, a tank crewman's helmet on his head. His gaunt face and bugged eyes gave him a hideous look, like a warrior from another planet around another star in another time in celestial history, and in the shadows behind him the figure in the dim light of a military tank, its 4 inch gun glowing from the heat of its firings, and swinging around to be aimed directly at us. As soon as we understood, some of us dropped to the ground, others fled through the heavy swinging entrance door into the light of day. Several of them vomited, others ran to the protective railing of the bridge, and looked down, then looked skyward with terrified faces, hardly aware of each other. All looking as though they had had a deep grinding look at Hell. What, what, oh Dear God, what what? What is the truth of all this, what is the Dorian picture trying to tell us? Please, dear God, help us, help us, god oh god . . . Probably all we know, is that it all had something to do with communism, terrorism, Karl Marx, socialism, the free market, investment, the stock market, stolen elections, corporate control of almost all the money everywhere, the rest of us, well, oh what can you expect, we don't matter, it looks like this, maybe in the few years left before it is all over anyway, with all the money burning in hell, great roaring flames as huge mountains of paper money explode and roar in great orange and purple clouds and spread toward every horizon, while there is still time to burn, time to burn, at last, still time to burn . . . Then in the desolation that follows, there is the original pristine portrait of Dorian America, and in the desolation and staggering wreckage of everything everywhere in sight, is the tank commander's figure, [Photograph: a young girl.] `The end of the world?' Yeh, so what? At least it'll be our ball game for the last century. Get out of my face, man. We know what we're doing'.
