A Shoshone drum

The voice of a Shoshone drum is the heartbeat of native peoples, of every age, every century, every place. It is the pulse of every animal in the earth, the surge of sap in every tree, to tell us to be faithful to the rhythm of the rains, the rivers, the coming of the sun, the passing of the sun, the coming of the moon. In our knowledge we are lost unless we listen to the wisdom of the trees, the wisdom of the animals in the earth and in the seas.
