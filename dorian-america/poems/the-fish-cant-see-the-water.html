The fish can't see the water

That's how you get used to something. You look at things as you swim around. It always looks the same, except at night, when it gets opaque gray with pollution, but after a while that's your world. Air is like water. We swim in it, like fish swim in water. We do not see the air. Fish do not see the water. We are told some things are true. We do not know that - we are only told it. We do not see them, or know where they come from, we do not see the air, fish do not see the water. If water is polluted when fish are born, it looks like the truth to them. That's the only way they know it. When we are born into poisoned air, we do not see it. It is all we know, it looks like the truth. Fish do not see the water. We do not see the air. We live and die in air and water we do not see.
