The third cross

The third cross tells it all. It says the Christian crusades are in your front yard, are in your town, and that you got to go. You're black or dark or aboriginal and you got to go. Remember your daddy 15 years ago. After the third cross he got tarred and feathered by the Christian crusade to keep holiness and the bible and heaven clean and nice clear white, cause that's what the Ku Klux preachers said the bible said. Your daddy, after 2 months he was still getting little patches of tar off, some with little itchy pieces of chicken feathers. Then they tell of 6 little girls hiding under mother's hoop skirt while the Klan circled like executioners because your daddy was Seminole in a town on a continent where he didn't belong but where he'd been ten thousand years and that awful night when daddy was maybe dying and mama hitched up the mules in the rain and tried to get all of us to the doctor in town for daddy and luck was with us for once when we got across the bridge with the mules about 7 minutes before the wild creek washed it down river. Maybe it was the white dove we saw in the bridge rafters, that maybe was God for a little while. Then the doctor came the next morning to the machine shop on the edge of town where you all had to hide soaking wet and somebody maybe the white dove brought some food and old Doc Morrissey said he had good news and bad news. Tell me the good news first mama said, and Doc said well he doesn't have typhoid, that's the good news but they burned your house down to the ground only the pot belly stove's still there, that's the bad news - all in the rain. Where, where dear Lord was your white dove? Take this nightmare cross from us tonight, this third cross stabbed in the earth before our little place on the earth. God have mercy, let these crusades pass, let these circling Christian murderers pass, these nightmares dressed in white who are our neighbors who hate us because Bill is Seminole, because he's been here 10,000 years. Oh, white dove, help us, let us have peace and mercy white dove, please sit on the third cross and put a spell on it so it sinks into the earth, into its own everlasting grave, never again to let third hooded crosses circle the ruins of our home, the shelter of our 6 little sisters who live with us, and let the white dove bless them to still be able to love and share laughter when the're grown women, and will never, ever again see or have to think of a third cross stabbed in the earth.
