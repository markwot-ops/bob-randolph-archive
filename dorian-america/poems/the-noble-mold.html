The noble mold

The noble mold on German white grapes, or Hungarian Tokays, leaving them discolored, an odor of rot, like stale unwashed clothing. yet sheltered within the chemistries are all the derivative liquids of lives of older ones; of grapes or souls, at last touching the threshold of a nectared myrrh, delicately poised a time before their death, to give a liquid perfume so ethereal and sweetly dear it can only be harvested in the immediate hours before their deaths, like the blended vintage lives of our elders - at the very last yielding up the perfect sense of life - seen only at this harvest time of gently dying lives, of grapes, of rotting fruit - of sweetly fermenting lives the time of life that is that richest, loveliest magic gift that must contain this mold of age - to let us have the richest sip of life, occurring only in the ripest time , the time of twinkling eyes underneath our shaggy brows and wrinkled cheeks so like the vines along the Danube and the Rhine, in the season of colored leaves so green and gray and orange and red - in the last days before the time of winter's silence - this noble mold must be earned, through the spring and the summertime, through the chilling dawns of autumn, to give us near the end those gorgeous spatlese, auslese, beerenauslese and trockenbeerenauslese wines - - summing up our lives.
