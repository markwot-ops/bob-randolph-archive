HIROSHIMA

Such ancient agriculture nestling in the hills. Old houses, old peasant men, women already in the fields. Ground moisture in the glades, green with rice planting. The air hazy hot under the pale sky. Today is August 6, 1962 in Hiroshima. Breakfast in Hiroshima; tomato juice, ham, pancakes, Log Cabin Syrup. How unexpected - Log Cabin Syrup - a uniquely American thing in this city. Outside the crowd gathers. The memorial crowd is quiet in Hiroshima. Today is August 6, 1945 in Hiroshima. The all clear sounds, after a restless, fitful night; Alarms rudely shaking sleep's cradle. Today Hiroshima is safe, the B-29s droned on to other targets. August 6th - Monday - a new week begins. So tired; God, to be so tired. Hiroshima stirs, figures drift back from the sheltering hills. Eight o'clock; women and children eating in the huts, in the dark lean-tos. The streets full of people; bicycles and cars, wagons and carts circling down narrow streets. The crowd grew, the children's chorus sings memorial music... A hundred boys and girls - in yellow berets. Very warm, bits of pale clouds, masses of heat clouds on the horizon. The memorial crowd is quiet, in Hiroshima. Very warm. Is every August 6th this warm in Hiroshima? Narrow shops on narrow streets, proprieters perspiring. Sultry huts... no ventilation Wreathes of flowers are placed on the memorial...the memorial music... somber, haunting. The city hums. More wreathes, one by one. Liz puts a wreath on for the Americans. Three B-29s appear out of the pale southeast. The people of Hiroshima ignore the planes. They're used to lone planes, or twos, or threes. It's the big raids they fear. Speeches now. "We hope that all the peoples on this earth will..." The B-29s move gracefully over Hiroshima. Nearly overhead. Something drops, a parachute opens, swinging gently down, ever so gently. Hundreds of doves sweep overhead, released as symbols of peace. The object is within sight. The doves circle. "...that all the peoples on this earth will find ways to live... "FLASH! 8:15! White! Pikadon! White Everyone! JESUS WHITE! BUDDHA WHITE! Evaporate! Gone! Gone! Gone. The memorial gong... 8:15. 40,000 people pay their devotion The clouds seem in attendance... on all sides. The memorial crowd is quiet, in Hiroshima. The screaming, everywhere pieces of human beings claw their way... Clothed in hanging rags of skin, Sizzling fat on open flesh. Children and babies underfoot like crisp bacon. Blind Japanese, some with eyeballs hanging... Stumbling over half-living children. The heat! The heat! Suddenly day is night, The sun a bloody disc in boiling darkness... The execution of 200,000 human beings; with care and method; the 'target' - Hiroshima, history's greatest gas oven. How unexpected, a uniquely American thing in this city... A uniquely American thing. The memorial crowd is quiet - in Hiroshima.
