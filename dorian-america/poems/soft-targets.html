Soft targets

Soft targets in the struggle for control we strike soft targets first - in Nicaragua, and everywhere else where the people are getting uppity. Soft targets first, most of all and last. Bombs in schoolrooms, we pay somebody to do it. Plenty of folks that will do it, their kids need everything. Poisoned candy for the Indians of the Amazon. Castrated priests, we can arrange all this. Plastic bombs in baby carriages, nuns whose vaginas have been scoured with machetes in mountain villages to make way for investments to keep wages paid by profitable businesses low, get wages down at home. If profits are good at 60 cents an hour then they'll be better at 20 cents. Get our cash flow better, to set aside for takeovers and golden parachutes but get the vulgar ones who complain write poetry on prison walls, protest against our care we stand for freedom everywhere, but remember go after the softness. Go after easy ones, to change the atmosphere, let there be blood in the air, dead babies in the streets, behind the houses, once in a while make it look like "they" did it to themselves, truth itself is an easy soft target. Soft targets easily strangled to soften the valley the mountain passes dead women to soften husbands, kidnapped villagers murdered by police teach lessons to surviving soft targets, out in the public burned buildings, pillage soft targets. poison toothpaste and cigars for Fidel, publish CIA papers blame soft targets for their fates, plant lies and contraband to twist the minds of everyone to hide the truth from everyone to make soft targets of everyone of the earth turn the ferment from itself poison it with lies about the purposes of honorable revolutionaries who seek to break the grip of the deathly past. Honor is a soft target, trust between people in this continent is a soft target we must turn our victims' hatred toward each other. Leaflets from our planes saying anything to twist the minds of soft targets before we fire secret guns at their presidents, buy their parliaments at the market price, restore their shahs, their czars, their kaisers, their kings of Kuwait who serve us well we cannot care we destroy soft targets cut off testicles and heads in the night in the backs of Spanish-speaking police stations, sever hands suspend upside down with their heads in tubs of water - all in the name of the American way - the stubs of fingers, before their hands are around our throats snarling in our ears foul hard targets foul empire builders foul spoilers of our lives foul spoilers of our world we are no longer soft targets we did not want targets, soft or hard we wanted to love you and you to love us to be honest with each other, honesty - one of the highest forms of love you are our hard targets now, it was your idea. We would not kill your wives your mothers your babes as you have done to us for we are humanity speaking 2000 languages, we can be dammed up but not for long, not forever, our river is indestructible. You "railsplitter" that Neruda appealed to, who now turns out to be a builder of guillotines, not fence rails. When once we know who and what you are we will not believe your words, no longer trust your handshakes, you who see us as soft targets, you who are lost you who think of commodities and become commodities you who have declared soft target war against trees, against mineral deposits you who use beautiful beaches to test your armed landing craft you who have done such violence to us as we would never do to you. Soft targets, yes you who would make us soft targets, you who would make the defenseless world itself a soft target, a great target, you who think of targets square round targets, female-shaped transparent opaque oblong bell-shaped targets, targets targets always targets. Are your family members "targets"? Are your children targets in your scheme of things ?
