Our old friend Newt....

The next time the pendulum swings the other way, it will lop off Newt Gingrich's balls. Also, small thought - I understand he has incredible hemorrhoids. Red and swollen with some shades of purple on the under side, and kind of shiny. And sort of a green spot up above, the doctor thinks maybe it's fungus but they don't want to talk about it. It's too weird and with tiny white knobs too. Embarrassing if the word got out.
