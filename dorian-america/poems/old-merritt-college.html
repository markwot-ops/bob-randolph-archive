Old Merritt College

A great temple of life, the young - with laughter now only a burial temple of silence. Moldy silence in the dark tiny leaks of light through weathered places sounds of creatures scurrying in dead debris, dust long silent after girls in oxfords and skirts boys with notebooks hair greased and combed, smiling slightly toward the girls in white skirts and colored sweaters before boarded windows telephones that rang. Laughter in the cafeteria corridors of grime 20 years of grime, water stains students in the library a boy and a girl look at pictures together, all in the dim halls of the past, down the echoing corridors of age and neglect, in what could have been alive with later girls and boys, or symphonies or classes for old people in the neighborhood, or theater, or so much else but no, we are not so equipped to preserve our dear structures from our past. We have other priorities, all born of madness.
