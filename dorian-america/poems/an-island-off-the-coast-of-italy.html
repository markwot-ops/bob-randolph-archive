An island off the coast of Italy

In every sweetheart's dream, there is an island off the coast of Italy. Every shorebird has memories that echoed from an island off the coast of Italy. On the edge of every sunset on any ocean, there is such an island, and on the planet Venus long long ago, there was an island off the coast of Italy. In Casseoipeia, Andromeda, Cygnus the Swan and Ursa Major, there have been other islands off the coasts of other Italies. In my heart, even now that I am older, there will always be one, as the warm love I feel for you once swirled near birds' nests high on cliffs on an island off the coast of Italy. On the bleak chill coasts of Wales, Norway and Greenland, old men and old women have never seen an island off the coast of Italy, but they have dreamed of it gazing into the foam of their ale. Most of those who were born on an island off the coast of Italy are buried there, in a windswept graveyard that basks in the Mediterranean sun on summer days. An island off the coast of Italy is a place of living cliffs and green slopes, that still cannot believe it has that rare good fortune to be one of the islands off the coast of Italy.
