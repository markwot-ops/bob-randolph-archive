Burger King

(As many already know, the fast food chains get their beef from other, cheaper places in the world; for example, Central America, where land and labor are cheaper, but to graze cattle you must clear rainforests.) A hamburger for $2.50 is very special at Burger King, for in it are azure blue butterflies, tomatoes, giant cicadas, onions, bits of jaguars and anacondas twenty feet long, great lilies and wild orchids, Some beef, a few Venus fly traps, and 8000 species that haven't been catalogued; including the mold on the Alarana berries in the rainforests of Panama that would have been the cure for cancer had we found it in time.... Then add some ground up laughing monkeys and lavender parrots, for a little variety. The land was cleared to graze for beef for 10 years until the soil erodes and has no nourishment in Costa Rica. But never mind, there's always Venezuela and Brazil, and eventually Canada and California. So, for now..... just put a little more mustard on, maybe it will cover up the funny taste.
