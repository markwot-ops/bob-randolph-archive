Those who play guitars must win

There is so little time for music in our veins - only 60 or 70 or 80 years and so many are tearing the veins of the world out by the roots, as they tear guitar strings out with military equipment meant for the purpose. Conquer a people's music and you conquer the soul of their world. You strip off the harmonies of the people and you drink their blood while they play love songs late at night in public parks or on beaches beside the soft lapping sound of baby waves. Those who play guitars must win. You seek to silence ordinary sounds of ordinary people making ordinary love in ordinary places to the caresses of octaves and arpeggios softened by candlelight while the Souza troops goosestep into a bleeding military future. Those who play guitars must win. But those of us who cry through flutes and violins and guitars have such long histories. We are like sinewy rock formations that endured every fiery heat and have emerged with the beauty of our layers intact, and we have survived. Those who play guitars must win. We again beat drums beat drums sing angel choruses that will deafen cannons and turn them into themselves to explode in their own mouths, within the hearing of lovers singing choruses of songs from Venus, heated Mercury, the lonely moons of Saturn and Neptune from all the spaces of the goddess. Those who play guitars must win. Ours is the goddess that would seize the ears of juvenile bullies and twist them til they throw down their guns and they will then pray for help from singers and minstrels who told their tales for centuries through the wars that left military heroes ground into the sands of ancient cities. Those who play guitars must win So the great cries of life in every key, accompanying every dance, every whirling gripping movement of making love to our own music we give the world. The love that is our music, the music that is our love, with finally no room left for military dismemberment of fools in their pride of state so foully seeking dominion over guitars guitars safe with everlasting life, to let those who play guitars be heard. Those who play guitars must win.
